Conventions:
- "11 flows" means the 11 pinned flows. "5 flows" means the coordinator's 5 flows. "headline" means
  `standards_token_transfer_private_to_private`.
- "min of N" means min wall over N interleaved reps (`ab.sh`) unless stated otherwise. A ratio is B/A (new/old).
- Peak RSS is `getrusage` maxrss of `bb` unless stated otherwise.
- Machine: "4 vCPU" means the author said only "4 vCPU VM". IFMA is marked only where the author stated it. The coordinator VM
  has no IFMA (final/README: "4 vCPU Intel Xeon @ 2.80GHz, no AVX-512 IFMA").
- Bases: `start` = bb15fcb + 0001-0006; integ2, integ3 and integ5 are the coordinator's integrated series (STATUS).

How the final2 patches map to track patches comes from final-wip/README, final/README, notes and the patch messages.

  integ3/0002. integ 0014 and 0035 (the background parser and its removal) cancel out.
- r3-memory/0001 (final2 0069) replaces r3-sumcheck-algo/0001.
- r3-msm-algo/0001 (a test compile fix) is folded into 0038.
